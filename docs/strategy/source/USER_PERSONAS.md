# AERYO User Personas

Version: 1.0

---

# Purpose

Aeryo is not built for everyone.

This document defines the users we optimize for.

When making product decisions, prioritize the Primary Persona first.

---

# Primary Persona

## The Progression Rider

### Profile

Age: 25-45

Experience:

- Beginner+
- Intermediate
- Advanced

Owns equipment.

Kites regularly.

Works full-time.

Limited free time.

Actively improving.

---

### Goals

- Maximize water time
- Avoid wasted trips
- Find the best local sessions
- Improve riding
- Discover better spots

---

### Frustrations

- Forecasts are difficult to interpret
- Too many apps required
- Conditions change unexpectedly
- Doesn't know which spot is best
- Wastes time checking multiple sources

---

### Current Workflow

Windy

↓

Windfinder

↓

WhatsApp

↓

Friends

↓

Instagram

↓

Guess

---

### Desired Outcome

Open Aeryo.

Know where to go.

Know why.

Leave.

---

### Success Metric

"I got more quality sessions this month."

---

# Secondary Persona

## The Explorer

### Profile

Travels for kitesurfing.

Adventure oriented.

Values experiences.

Interested in discovering new places.

---

### Goals

- Discover new destinations
- Plan better trips
- Find hidden opportunities
- Learn about locations

---

### Frustrations

- Information is fragmented
- Hard to compare destinations
- Local knowledge is difficult to access

---

### Desired Outcome

Find destinations with confidence.

---

# Tertiary Persona

## The Weekend Warrior

### Profile

Limited availability.

Family obligations.

Can only ride occasionally.

---

### Goals

- Maximize limited free time
- Never miss great conditions

---

### Desired Outcome

Know exactly when conditions are worth prioritizing.

---

# Users We Are NOT Optimizing For

## Social Media Users

People seeking entertainment.

Not a priority.

---

## Professional Athletes

Needs are too specialized.

Not a priority.

---

## Weather Enthusiasts

People primarily interested in weather data.

Not a priority.

---

## Marketplace Users

Buying and selling gear.

Not a priority.

---

# Core User Insight

The user does not want weather.

The user wants confidence.

The user wants to know:

"Is it worth going?"

Everything should support answering that question.
